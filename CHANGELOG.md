## 0.1.0 — 2026-09-16

- Add the experimental quick-cook journey with continuous egg size, starting temperature and Soft/Jammy/Firm texture controls.
- Estimate cooking time and water boiling temperature from a documented heating model and bounded pressure conversion.
- Add optional consented elevation, surface-pressure and locality lookup with standard, altitude and offline fallbacks.
- Add the animated egg, absolute-time countdown, generated sound, final-ten-second ticks, persistent Ready reminder and retained stopped result.
- Add a clearly marked accelerated demo, reduced-motion behavior and strict same-tab recovery for real cooks.
- Add responsive, keyboard and accessibility behavior verified across Chromium, Firefox and WebKit.
- Document privacy, source/data licensing, architecture, release operations and the evolution from discovery to an engineered MVP.

Known limitations: cooking times remain uncalibrated estimates; Soft and Jammy are not fully cooked; Firm is not a pasteurization guarantee; browser suspension can prevent alarms; offline reopening is not supported. Kitchen validation, long-term personal-use observations and broader actual-device coverage remain future work.
