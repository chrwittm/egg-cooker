## Media Lab experiment

The separate web-media experiment uses the shipped rooster unchanged for its immediate sound check. Four mono 44.1 kHz MP3 derivatives in `src/experiments/assets/` prepend 60 or 300 seconds of silence or original soft synthesized ticks before the same recording. Their duration, SHA-256 and source identity are recorded in [tracks.json](src/experiments/assets/tracks.json). The FFmpeg generator is `scripts/generate-media-tracks.mjs`; no decoder or new audio dependency is shipped. These files test background playback, not accepted native or suspended-alarm functionality.
