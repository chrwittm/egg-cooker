# Product context

**Status:** The maintainer accepted the current quick-cook MVP and its interaction refinements on 2026-09-16 for an experimental 0.1.0 release. The living MVP is reconciled with the hands-on review; later behavior is additional feature work. Implementation and automated verification are recorded in [delivery evidence](../delivery/2026-09-16-texture-location-verification.md). Kitchen calibration and broader actual-device evidence remain explicit backlog work, not release claims.

- **User and outcome:** A person making eggs wants a simple, engaging cooking instrument with minimal cognitive load. An original animated egg cross-section changes alongside the calculated timeline.
- **Design principle:** Simplicity first. Values, tactile choices, the egg and one primary action; detailed science, operating assumptions and failure explanations in contextual disclosures. Retain accessible labels and informed data sharing.
- **Current MVP:** Two screens, Configure → Cook. S/M/L/XL presets and grams; fridge **8 °C**, room **20 °C** and temperature slider; continuous texture with Soft/Jammy/Firm highlights across midpoint-defined ranges and centered anchors and M/Fridge/Soft defaults; requested local elevation/weather surface pressure and optional city and pressure on one line in a separated Location data group; time plus water boiling temperature; animated countdown; direct Back; final-ten-second ticks and persistent Ready sound; Ready/Stop timer/Cook another egg on Cook; a shared egg slides between states in one phone-width column. Elevation and surface-pressure sliders; clearly labeled demo defaults on during testing, with 1×/10×/20×/50× controls and automatic 1× at Ready.
- **Deployment boundary:** Static GitHub Pages. Calculations/state/animation run in the browser; consented requests call public elevation/weather and optional client-side locality APIs directly. Standard and altitude fallbacks remain available; the loaded cooking journey works without network. No backend, proxy, secrets, accounts or alternate hosting.
- **Accepted contract:** [0001 — MVP](specifications/0001-mvp.md), [science and pressure model](specifications/0001-mvp-science.md). Steam and immersion share a boiling-boundary approximation. Doneness mappings and method equivalence remain unvalidated in a kitchen; mathematical fixtures are not cooking trials.
- **Deferred:** [0002 — Guided cooking](specifications/0002-guided-cooking.md), calibration/feedback, history/preferences and the other named non-goals remain deferred. There is no quantity, method or mode picker.
- **Data:** Validated real active-cook snapshots use `sessionStorage`; Stop timer/Back/Cook another egg delete them. A separate boolean automatic-location preference uses localStorage; known browser permission enables one refresh on later visits. Coordinates rounded to three decimals are shared with Open-Meteo (conditions) and BigDataCloud (optional locality) after informed activation or an enabled, already-permitted repeat visit, never persisted or logged by application code. No city names, raw API responses, history or demo sessions are persisted. Provider request logging is separate.
- **Platform/evidence:** English, grams/Celsius, EU categories. Production tests cover Chromium/Firefox/WebKit; that does not certify iPhone/Android audio or suspended execution. Same-tab recovery is best-effort. Offline reopening/PWA is deferred.
- **Release:** Experimental 0.1.0 candidate prepared; no source push, tag, release or Pages deployment performed yet. Hosted-origin smoke testing follows deployment. Kitchen calibration and broader actual-device evidence are post-0.1.0 backlog work; public language must continue to identify estimates and platform limits. See the [index and delivery ledger](specifications/README.md).

The stack remains [ADR 0001](../architecture/decisions/0001-baseline.md); execution lives in [status](../planning/status.md), priority in the [backlog](../planning/backlog.md).
