# Product context

**Status:** Experimental v0.1.2 is published and deployed. The bounded language, identity and alarm polish from [0005](specifications/0005-release-polish.md) passed the local and Linux release gates plus foreground Safari/Home Screen acceptance on an iPhone 16 Pro with iOS 26.6.2 on 2026-09-17. The unpublished v0.1.1 candidate remains preserved after its Linux gate failure. Kitchen calibration and broader actual-device evidence remain explicit backlog work, not release claims.

- **User and outcome:** A person making eggs wants a simple, engaging cooking instrument with minimal cognitive load. An original animated egg cross-section changes alongside the calculated timeline.
- **Design principle:** Simplicity first. Values, tactile choices, the egg and one primary action; detailed science, operating assumptions and failure explanations in contextual disclosures. Retain accessible labels and informed data sharing.
- **Current MVP:** Two screens, Configure → Cook. S/M/L/XL presets and grams; fridge **8 °C**, room **20 °C** and temperature slider; continuous texture with Soft/Jammy/Firm highlights across midpoint-defined ranges and centered anchors and M/Fridge/Soft defaults; requested local elevation/weather surface pressure and optional city and pressure in a matching Location data surface; time plus water boiling temperature; animated countdown; direct Back; final-ten-second ticks and a bundled rooster Ready call with synthesized fallback; Ready/Stop timer/Cook another egg on Cook; a shared egg slides between states in one phone-width column. Complete English/German presentation, an explicit language preference and the Bold egg icon are included. Elevation and surface-pressure sliders; clearly labeled demo defaults on during testing, with 1×/10×/20×/50× controls and automatic 1× at Ready.
- **Deployment boundary:** Static GitHub Pages. Calculations/state/animation run in the browser; consented requests call public elevation/weather and optional client-side locality APIs directly. Standard and altitude fallbacks remain available; the loaded cooking journey works without network. No backend, proxy, secrets, accounts or alternate hosting.
- **Accepted contract:** [0001 — MVP](specifications/0001-mvp.md), [science and pressure model](specifications/0001-mvp-science.md). Steam and immersion share a boiling-boundary approximation. Doneness mappings and method equivalence remain unvalidated in a kitchen; mathematical fixtures are not cooking trials.
- **Deferred:** [0002 — Guided cooking](specifications/0002-guided-cooking.md), calibration/feedback, history/preferences and the other named non-goals remain deferred. There is no quantity, method or mode picker.
- **Data:** Validated real active-cook snapshots use `sessionStorage`; Stop timer/Back/Cook another egg delete them. Separate validated local preferences hold the automatic-location boolean and an explicit `en`/`de` choice. Known browser permission enables one location refresh on later visits. Coordinates rounded to three decimals are shared with Open-Meteo (conditions) and BigDataCloud (optional locality) after informed activation or an enabled, already-permitted repeat visit, never persisted or logged by application code. BigDataCloud receives the active language only with that authorized request. No city names, raw API responses, history or demo sessions are persisted. Provider request logging is separate.
- **Platform/evidence:** English/German, grams/Celsius, EU categories. Production tests cover Chromium/Firefox/WebKit; that does not certify iPhone/Android audio or suspended execution. Same-tab recovery is best-effort. The manifest/icon set makes no offline/PWA or native-timer promise.
- **Release:** [Experimental 0.1.2](https://github.com/chrwittm/egg-cooker/releases/tag/v0.1.2) is published from tagged source `6fb8d2f` and deployed to the [live app](https://chrwittm.github.io/egg-cooker/). v0.1.1 remains an unpublished rejected candidate after its Linux gate failed. Kitchen calibration and broader actual-device evidence remain backlog work; public language must continue to identify estimates and platform limits.
